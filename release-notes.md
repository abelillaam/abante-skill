## What's new in 1.0.3

- Your daily goal now updates live while you practice, and shows your time the moment you leave a lesson
- Practice time counts by the second, so short sessions add up instead of being lost
- Only real practice counts: time pauses on other tabs, when the app is in the background, or when your phone is locked
- Reading a lesson's theory cards now counts toward your goal too
- Each account has its own daily minutes; switching accounts on the same phone no longer mixes them
- Your daily minutes are saved to your account, so they follow you to another phone
